📖 How to Use

Create and share gap-fill listening exercises quickly.

1. Upload a video

Drag and drop or click. Any common format works.

2. Wait for transcript

The transcript appears automatically.

3. Edit gaps

  • Click any word to make it a gap (turns yellow).
  • Click a yellow word to remove the gap.
  • "✏️ Edit Text" button — fix transcript mistakes. Your gaps are preserved.

4. Generate Gaps

AI fills in gaps based on the level you choose.

5. Share with students

  • Preview — See what students will see.
  • Student Link — Copy and send. No signup needed.
  • Save Exercise — Sign in with Google to sync across devices.

How students use it

  1. Video plays the first sentence automatically.
  2. They type the missing words.
  3. They click ✅ Submit — green ✓ correct, red ✗ wrong.
  4. Next sentence loads after 6 seconds.
  5. At the end they see their score.
